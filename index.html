How it works

Four moments, in order

  1. 01
    An agent reaches a consequential step

    Anything that spends money, messages a human, or destroys data pauses instead of proceeding.

  2. 02
    Your phone gets the ask

    A push notification with exactly what the agent wants to do and why, readable in five seconds.

  3. 03
    You approve or decline

    One tap. The agent continues, or it doesn't. No terminal, no dashboard hunt.

  4. 04
    The decision is sealed

    Who asked, what you decided, what happened next. A permanent entry in your ledger.

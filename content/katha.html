Here to listen, or to hear a sample first? Head to katha.syahi.sh.

I The method is the pitch
  1. Agents produce

    Narration, pronunciation, pacing, chapter marks. The production line is agentic end to end, which is how a catalog gets through it.

  2. Every sentence checked

    Each recorded sentence is verified against the printed text. Not spot-checked. Checked. A dropped line or a wrong number fails the title, not the listener.

  3. A human signs off

    Nothing ships on a machine's say-so. A person approves the title, and the sign-off leaves a receipt.

Agents draft, a person signs, the record keeps the receipt.

II Who it's for

For

  • Rights-holders with audio-dark catalogs
  • Publishers who want titles out, not a project
  • Ed-techs with course texts worth hearing
  • Anyone who needs the audio faithful to the page
III The ask

Bring a catalog, or one title you care about. We'll tell you what it takes to produce it, faithfully and disclosed, and where it can land. Titles go to the audiobook stores that welcome disclosed AI narration; the exact list is named per title, in writing, before production starts.
